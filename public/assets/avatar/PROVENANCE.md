# Avatar provenance

`demo-rig.glb` is generated by `scripts/export-demo-avatar.ts` from original
geometry and clips in `src/lib/avatar-rig.ts`. License: project MIT license.
No external model or real person's likeness was used. All motions are
engineering fixtures, not reviewed ISL. The default rig is constructed at runtime.

Place licensed compatible GLB/GLTF models here and preserve their own source,
license, attribution, consent, and review records. Do not apply the project MIT
license to third-party assets unless their actual rights permit that.
